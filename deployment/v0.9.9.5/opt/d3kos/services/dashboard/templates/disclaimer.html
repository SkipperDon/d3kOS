d3kOS — Experimental Software Notice

EXPERIMENTAL SOFTWARE

d3kOS is experimental software. It is provided at your own risk and is not a replacement for the captain's judgement, professional seamanship, or any flag-state approved navigation system.

The software assumes you are operating a power-driven vessel under 20 m. If your vessel is different, some advisory outputs may not apply. You can change the vessel type and length class in Setup.

SENSOR LIMITATIONS

d3kOS has no radar. It receives AIS position reports only. Vessels without AIS are completely invisible to it — including the majority of small craft, sailing boats, tenders, kayaks, swimmers, and floating debris.

SafeHelm is one input among many. You must never depend on it alone. Silence from the system does not mean clear water; it means no qualifying AIS target was detected within the configured range gates.

NOT A LOOK-OUT

d3kOS is not a look-out and does not satisfy the look-out requirement of COLREGs Rule 5. A proper look-out by sight and hearing is always required.

NOT ARPA / NOT TYPE-APPROVED

SafeHelm Collision Advisory is not an ARPA (Automatic Radar Plotting Aid) and is not type-approved under any IMO performance standard, flag-state requirement, or classification society rule. It does not meet the requirements of SOLAS Chapter V for collision avoidance equipment.

CAPTAIN'S RESPONSIBILITY

The captain remains solely responsible for the safe navigation of the vessel at all times, including collision avoidance, keeping a proper look-out, and compliance with the International Regulations for Preventing Collisions at Sea. d3kOS provides advisory information only; it does not issue commands and does not override the captain's authority or duty.

ACCEPTANCE

By tapping ACCEPT you confirm that you have read and understood this notice. Your acceptance will be recorded along with the current d3kOS version and a hash of this text so that any future change to these terms requires a fresh acknowledgement.
