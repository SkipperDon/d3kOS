§1Introduction

d3kOS is a marine navigation hub that runs on a Raspberry Pi mounted at your helm. It integrates chart navigation (AvNav), engine monitoring (NMEA 2000 via Signal K), AI-powered vessel assistance (Helm), camera monitoring (Marine Vision), weather overlays, predictive maintenance, and a boat log — all accessible from a single touchscreen interface.

What d3kOS is not: it is not a standalone chartplotter. It works alongside your existing MFD and VHF radio. It adds an AI intelligence layer and centralised monitoring that your MFD does not provide.

First Boot

On first power-up, d3kOS launches the Setup Wizard automatically. Complete all 8 steps before the main dashboard becomes accessible. The wizard saves your vessel, engine, and electronics configuration — the AI uses this information to provide accurate diagnostics specific to your boat.

A QR code appears. On your phone, open atmyboat.com/app, log in, tap Pair Device, and scan the QR code. The QR code encodes your Pi's unique device token. Tap SKIP THIS STEP to pair later from Settings → Mobile.

Step 8 — Ready to Set Sail

All configuration complete. Tap LAUNCH d3kOS › — the wizard saves all data and loads the main dashboard.

§4Weather Panel

Windy — Wind & Wave Map

Embedded Windy.com map showing wind speed, direction, and wave height overlays centred on your GPS position. Pinch to zoom, drag to pan. Defaults to wave height overlay — tap the map menu to switch to wind, pressure, rain, etc. Wind in knots, temperature in °C. Requires internet.

Radar

Embedded RainViewer radar map showing precipitation. Animated — shows the last 2 hours of radar frames. Requires internet.

Switching Tabs

Two tabs at the top: WINDY and RADAR. Tap to switch. Both maps lazy-load on first open.

§5Marine Vision — Camera System

Camera Grid

Main Marine Vision screen shows a grid of all configured camera slots. Each cell displays a live feed (if connected), camera name, and a status indicator: green (live), amber (connecting), red (offline). Tap any cell to open it full-screen — pinch to zoom, Record button to start/stop clip recording, Back to return to grid.

Camera Slots

Cameras are assigned to named slots (Bow, Stern, Port, Starboard, Helm, etc.) in Settings → Cameras. Up to 8 slots supported. Slots without a physical camera assigned show a placeholder.

Fish Detection

When a camera is active, d3kOS runs a YOLOv8-based AI model looking for fish in the frame. On detection: a FISH DETECTED banner appears, species identification queries the AI, species name and habitat notes appear, and the detection is logged with timestamp and confidence level. Runs at low priority — does not affect stream performance.

Forward Watch — AI Collision Avoidance

A bow-mounted camera configured as Forward Watch analyses the video feed for marine hazards (approaching vessel, person in water, kayak, debris). On detection: a HAZARD DETECTED alert fires, object type and estimated distance display, and GPS coordinates are pushed to your chartplotter as an AIS-like target. Forward Watch is a safety aid — it does not replace a proper watch or VHF radio.

Recordings

Camera clips stored at /home/d3kos/camera-recordings/. Named with camera slot and timestamp. Manage storage from Settings → Cameras → Storage.

AI Engine Diagnostic

Tap the AI DIAGNOSTIC button (or tap any cell in alert state on the main dashboard) to send current engine readings to Helm for analysis. Helm assesses the data against your engine's specification and provides a plain-language status report or recommended action. Available on T2 and T3 only.

Predictive Health Panel

CellWhat it shows
Advisory EventsCount of advisory-level threshold crossings this month
Critical EventsCount of critical-level events this month
Last EventType and timestamp of the most recent alert
TrendAI assessment: Stable / Declining / Improving

d3kOS logs every engine snapshot to a local database. The predictive service analyses these logs continuously. When it detects a developing problem — coolant trending up over multiple sessions, oil pressure gradually declining — it fires a predictive alert before readings reach a threshold. Alerts appear on the dashboard ticker, are spoken by Helm, sent to your phone (T1+), and logged in your AtMyBoat account.

Example predictive alert: "Coolant temperature has averaged 4°C higher than your baseline over the last 3 sessions. Recommend checking coolant level and inspecting the raw water impeller before your next voyage."

§7Helm Assistant — AI Chat

Helm is your AI First Mate. It understands your vessel, engine, location, and current instrument state.

Starting a Conversation

Voice: Tap the HELM button on the navigation bar. The "HELM IS LISTENING" overlay appears. Speak naturally — Helm uses Vosk for speech recognition (runs locally, no internet required for transcription). Piper TTS (Amy — female voice) reads the response aloud.

Text: In the Helm Assistant page (More → Helm Assistant), type your question and tap Send.

What You Can Ask

Helm has context about your vessel name, type, engine, specifications, current GPS position, speed, course, engine readings, active navigation route, boat log entries, and any documents you have uploaded.

Example questions:

  • "What's my ETA to the next waypoint at current speed?"
  • "My coolant is running at 98°C — is that normal for my engine?"
  • "What should I check if my oil pressure drops below 40 PSI?"
  • "What is the legal minimum life jacket requirement in Ontario?"
  • "What are the bag limits for walleye in Lake Erie?"

Mute / Pause

Tap the mute button (speaker icon, top-right of Helm screen) to toggle TTS voice on or off. Mute state is saved across reboots. Helm pauses automatically when you record a voice note in the Boat Log, then resumes.

Documents

Helm can answer questions from documents you have uploaded (vessel manuals, engine guides, fishing regulations). Go to More → Upload Documents to add files.

§8Boat Log

Your onboard journal — records engine snapshots, voice notes, text entries. Exports to CSV or JSON.

Adding a Voice Note

  1. Tap Boat Log in the navigation bar
  2. Tap the microphone button
  3. Speak — recording stops automatically after a pause, or tap again to stop
  4. Helm transcribes the note using Vosk (local — no internet required)
  5. Transcribed text appears and is saved automatically with a timestamp

Engine Entries

Engine data is captured automatically every 30 minutes when the engine is running: RPM, coolant temperature, oil pressure, voltage, and fuel flow. Engine entries also trigger on significant events — if coolant exceeds the advisory threshold, an entry is written automatically.

Viewing and Exporting

Entries appear in reverse chronological order. Tap any entry to expand it. Tap Export to download as CSV (spreadsheet-compatible, with unit metadata) or JSON (full structured data).

§10Fix My Pi & OTA Updates

Fix My Pi

Remote diagnostic and repair service. Access from the d3kOS mobile app → Fix My Pi tab, or from atmyboat.com/fix-my-pi.

How it works:

  1. Describe the problem (e.g. "Dashboard shows blank screen", "Engine data stopped updating")
  2. Submit — queued in the AtMyBoat command queue
  3. Pi syncs within 15–30 minutes and pulls the command
  4. d3kOS runs automated diagnostics across all services
  5. A PDF report is generated and delivered to your Chart Room
TierFix My Pi cost
T1$29.99 per incident
T2 / T3Included — unlimited

Can fix remotely: stopped services, configuration corruption, disk space, network problems, software crashes.
Cannot fix remotely: physical hardware failures, power supply problems, GPS antenna disconnections, camera hardware faults.

OTA Software Updates

Over-the-Air updates for T1, T2, and T3 subscribers. No SD card removal or keyboard required.

  1. In the app: Settings → System → Check for Updates
  2. If available, version number and release notes are shown
  3. Tap Install Update — update is queued
  4. Pi downloads and installs on next sync — typically 5–10 minutes
  5. Pi reboots once — app shows "Update complete" when back online
OTA updates never touch your personal data, vessel configuration, boat log, or API keys.

§13Remote Access

Shows the status of d3kOS's connection to the AtMyBoat.com monitoring service.

When connected, AtMyBoat.com receives: vessel name and approximate GPS position (last known — not a live track), system health status, engine data summaries (trend data — not raw readings), alert events (type and timestamp).

StatusMeaning
Connected (green)Data is flowing to AtMyBoat.com
Offline (amber)Pi has no internet — data queued for next connection
Disconnected (red)Connection error — check network settings
Privacy: GPS coordinates sent as approximate position only (±500m). T0 users can disable cloud sync entirely — d3kOS will never contact AtMyBoat.com. Control what data is shared in Settings → Remote Access.

§14Autonomous Health System

Six autonomous agents run continuously in the background (v0.9.8+).

AgentFunction
AA1 — Service WatchdogMonitors all 10 critical d3kOS services every 5 minutes. Restarts any stopped service. Alerts AtMyBoat.com if a service cannot be restarted.
AA2 — Log RotationPrevents log files from filling the SD card. Rotates and compresses old logs automatically.
AA3 — Health ReporterSends system health summaries to AtMyBoat.com. Generates the health score in the mobile app.
AA4 — Update CheckerChecks for available OTA updates and notifies the mobile app. Does not install without your confirmation.
AA5 — Network ResilienceMonitors WiFi and cellular. Reconnects automatically on drop. Switches connections if configured.
AA6 — Backup AgentBacks up vessel config, boat log, and engine history to USB at /mnt/usb-backup when a drive is inserted. Idle if no USB drive.

Health Score

The mobile app shows a Health Score (0–100). Reflects services running, recent failures, disk space, last sync, and active alerts. Score of 100 = everything normal. Below 80 = investigate. Below 50 = at least one critical service failed.

Failure Intelligence

When a service fails and cannot be auto-recovered, d3kOS generates a Failure Report sent to AtMyBoat.com. The report includes which service failed, error logs, actions attempted, and recommended repair steps. Appears in Account → Diagnostics. T1+ users can initiate Fix My Pi directly from the report.

§15Troubleshooting

Dashboard doesn't load / blank screen

The dashboard runs at http://localhost:3000 — Chromium connects directly to Flask. If you changed a template file, allow 30 seconds for Flask to restart. Check systemctl is-active d3kos-dashboard.

Instrument cells show "---"

Signal K is not receiving data from your NMEA gateway. Check: gateway is powered on and connected; Signal K server status at http://[pi-ip]:8099/admin; baud rate matches your DIP switch setting from Step 5 of the wizard.

Next Waypoint shows "No active route"

No route is active in AvNav. Open AvNav from the More menu → load a route → tap Activate → return to dashboard (updates within 15 seconds).

GPS position not showing

Ensure your GPS source is connected and transmitting to Signal K. If indoors, GPS will not get a fix — go outside with sky view. Check Signal K data browser for navigation.position — if it shows null, the GPS source is not connected.

Helm Assistant not responding / no voice

  • Check Gemini API key is set in Settings → AI Configuration
  • Check the USB microphone is plugged in and not muted — tap Helm and speak, check the waveform
  • For TTS silence: check Settings → AI Configuration → Voice Responses is not muted
  • Note: if Helm was muted before the last reboot, it stays muted. Check the mute state first.

Camera shows "Connecting" / offline

Check that the camera USB or RTSP source is connected. Check camera slot assignment in Settings → Cameras. Restart camera service: Settings → System → Reboot, or contact support.

Mobile app not connecting to Pi

  1. Confirm the Pi is paired — Settings → Mobile → check the paired devices list
  2. Check that the Pi has an internet connection — app communicates through AtMyBoat.com when you're away from the boat
  3. If Pi is online but showing as offline, wait 15 minutes for the next sync cycle
  4. If pairing is lost, re-pair from Settings → Mobile → Pair Device

Fix My Pi not completing

Fix My Pi requires the Pi to sync with AtMyBoat.com — Pi must have internet access. Check the Remote Access page to confirm the Pi is connected. If the fix completes but the problem persists, contact support at atmyboat.com/contact.

On-screen keyboard not appearing

The Squeekboard on-screen keyboard appears automatically on text input tap. If not: tap the field again. If behind the main window, check the bottom of the screen.

System time wrong after reboot

The Pi has no hardware clock. Time is set by NTP when internet is available (1–2 minutes after network connection). Offline: fake-hwclock keeps approximate time between reboots.

Health score below 80

Open the mobile app → tap Health Score to see which component is failing. Common causes: AA6 is amber if no USB drive inserted (insert one or ignore); a service stopped — watchdog will attempt restart; disk space low — clear trip data or export and clear the boat log.
